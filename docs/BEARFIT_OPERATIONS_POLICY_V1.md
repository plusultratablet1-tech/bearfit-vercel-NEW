# BearFit Operations & Policy Manual — V1

**Effective direction:** prospective for newly acknowledged bookings, 2026-11-14 (Asia/Manila).  
**Status:** owner-directed operational controls; money/coach-compensation exceptions remain **pending owner rate-policy approval**.  
**Applies to:** Members, Coaches, Staff, Admin, all branches.  
**Source of truth:** authenticated database records and Admin-audited decisions; not messages/screenshots alone.

> This policy does not retroactively change sold package terms, completed payroll, settled payments, or historical session credits. If a rule differs from a member's already-agreed contract, Admin must honor and document the applicable original terms.

## 1. Authority: who does what

| Action | Member | Assigned coach | Staff | Admin |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Package purchase / renewal | Request and pay | View only necessary client details | Record for review | Verify, approve, and activate |
| Set initial package expiry / restore credits | View and request correction | No | Escalate | Verify source and authorize audited change |
| Create availability | View | Plot available times and propose sessions | Manage slots | Oversee conflicts and final assignments |
| Booking | Request | Accept or report conflicts | Confirm assigned slot under policy | Resolve exceptions and overrides |
| Final schedule | **Acknowledge final date/time/coach** | View assigned booking | Follow up unconfirmed schedules | Resolve disputed assignment |
| Completed attendance | Attend / present verifiable proof | Verify, log factual attendance | Verify check-in | Audit and correct errors |
| No-show | Receive notice and dispute | **Report facts only; never charge** | Report facts | **Review evidence, waive, or authorize charge** |
| Package freeze | Submit dated proof | No | Assist filing | Approve within documented maximum |
| Coach pay | No | View payable conduct and dispute | Prepare | Approve/lock pay based on verified conducted work |
| Operational exception | Submit reason | Report reason | Record and escalate | Determine outcome and notify affected parties |

**Non-negotiable separation of duties:** the actor who reports an absence does not independently decide the member deduction. No coach can grant package extensions, edit member balances, or change payroll unilaterally.

## 2. Client lifecycle

1. **Register and verify identity.** Create one canonical member ID, branch, package, agreement version and emergency contact as appropriate. Avoid duplicate accounts. Do not activate a package merely because a payment was reported.
2. **Verify payment.** Admin reconciles amount, date and proof. Payment stage, outstanding balance and package activation must agree. Keep historical receipts.
3. **Issue package.** Store exact sessions, usage, starting date, actual validity, expiration and renewal terms. If original expiry is absent, Admin verifies the original agreement before any extension.
4. **Request sessions.** Member requests a suitable slot; coach can propose sessions, but that is not Admin confirmation. Pending requests never consume package sessions.
5. **Admin confirmation.** Staff/Admin checks coach availability, capacity, branch, service type, package eligibility and session balance.
6. **Member final acknowledgment.** After Admin confirms the exact assigned date, start/end time, coach, branch and training type, the member acknowledges the *final* booking. That acknowledgment is evidence of receipt, **not evidence of attendance**. If the assigned time/coach/service changes, the prior acknowledgment is no longer valid for the changed schedule.
7. **Session outcome.** Completed check-in/attendance consumes one package session once. No-show/late cancellation follows the separate evidence and review process. Cancellation by coach/BearFit consumes no member session.
8. **History and renewal.** Member can view charged/remaining sessions, cancellation notices, approved freezes, renewal/payment alerts, and request review of incorrect entries.

## 3. Cancellation and late arrivals

- **Newly accepted booking terms:** free self-cancellation closes at **10:00 PM Asia/Manila on the calendar day before the scheduled date**.
- **Existing bookings without new policy acceptance:** preserve their original **4-hour-before-start** cancellation right. Never switch an older booking to the stricter rule after its 10 PM cutoff has passed.
- **Within the applicable deadline:** a member cancellation is free. A cancellation request after the deadline enters Admin review; no automatic session deduction.
- **Member late arrival:** no arbitrary penalty in V1. Coach records arrival time and whether training actually occurred; Admin decides documented outcome using original agreement and verified facts. No invented minute threshold.
- **Member emergency/medical:** Admin records the member request, available evidence, decision and whether penalty is waived. Diagnosis should not be required. Keep sensitive details private.
- **Coach late/absent, equipment failure, gym closure, unsafe conditions or Admin scheduling error:** **never deduct the member** for BearFit's failure to deliver the session. Admin cancels/rebooks or offers another available time and records a reason. Member may choose a different coach rather than accept an unrequested replacement.
- **Double bookings/time changes:** Admin resolves capacity conflicts and asks member to re-confirm a changed booking.

## 4. Attendance and no-show protection

1. Only a valid confirmed booking can be investigated for a booking no-show; coach/staff report after the scheduled session ends.
2. A coach report is **not** an authorization to debit. Admin checks the booking terms, final client acknowledgment, check-in or QR record, any overlapping session logs, cancellation timestamp, coach feedback, branch records and credible member evidence.
3. Admin may charge **at most one session for one booking**, waive the charge, or dismiss the allegation with a written rationale; there is no automatic charge.
4. Charge notice appears in the authenticated member portal. The member may **acknowledge receipt** or **dispute**. Acknowledgment is not acceptance that the charge is correct.
5. Dispute window in V1 is **48 hours after first in-app viewing**; Admin can still manually investigate after the window. Never interpret silence as agreement or proof of absence.
6. Admin may restore a wrongly deducted session through an auditable correction without deleting the original session event. Coaches cannot restore sessions.
7. No-show session deductions **do not automatically count as coach-conducted sessions or create payroll**.

**Notification limitation:** current reminders and charge notices are **in-app**; do not promise email, SMS or push delivery until a delivery service and successful send log exist.

## 5. Strict freeze policy (existing BearFit V1)

- **One approved freeze per purchased package cycle**, minimum **3 consecutive calendar days**.
- **Travel:** dated booking ticket showing member and travel dates; request at least **7 days before** first affected day; max **7 days**.
- **Medical:** credible dated medical certificate stating inability to train, not diagnosis; request promptly within existing **72-hour** reporting condition; max **14 days**.
- Overall approved duration is also limited to the lesser of **14 days or 25% of verified original validity days** (rounded down).
- Admin must verify valid active package, date overlap, proof, available maximum and absence of conflicting confirmed sessions. No approval after expiration without documented owner exception and applicable old contract.
- Approval extends expiry only by approved calendar days. It never increases sessions or refunds money automatically. Freeze blocks training on those dates.
- All exceptions, originals, new expiration dates and Admin actor are recorded.

## 6. Coach availability and assigned work

- Coaches may publish availability or **propose** schedules for assigned clients. The proposal does not create a finalized appointment, payable conduction or deducted member session.
- Admin verifies capacity, coach specialization, member package and conflicts. A coach's “I'm available” is not client attendance or client final confirmation.
- Coach must notify Admin promptly of a clash/absence and submit the actual reason; cannot secretly cancel a client appointment or mark a client no-show to cover coach absence.
- Attendance/clock-in is not proof that a specific client completed a session. Conducted training must link to actual attendance evidence and, for booked sessions, the correct booking/member.

## 7. Compensation — preserve working ledgers, finalize separately

- **Current enforceable baseline:** ordinary conduction pay is based on verified **completed** sessions and Admin-approved rate snapshots/cutoffs. No-show, cancelled, pending and uncompleted bookings are **not automatically payable conducted sessions**.
- **Do not change** coach rate tables, retroactive payroll, cutoffs, locked/paid ledger entries, or financial adjustments as a consequence of this manual.
- **Owner approval still required** for: whether a coach receives an appearance/cancellation fee when a member no-shows; pay for partial training; standby time; travel-related absence; gym-initiated closure; substitute-coach split; and how exceptions affect commissions. Any approved compensation must be a distinct, reviewed payroll adjustment with a reason and proof, never a fabricated completed session.
- Until the owner signs these rates, hold the case for manual Admin payroll review, rather than create a member charge or coach payout automatically.

## 8. Admin exception matrix

| Incident | Member session | Coach ordinary conduction | Admin action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Verified attended training | Deduct exactly one | Eligible for review | Verify check-in/session record |
| Client no-show with signed terms and confirmed final schedule | At most one after evidence review | Not automatically payable | Decide and notify |
| Client late cancel | Review; no automatic debit | Not automatically payable | Record deadline/evidence; decide |
| Coach cancel / unavailable | No debit | No conducted pay by default | Record reason, notify, offer rebook |
| BearFit closure/safety failure | No debit | Owner sets exceptional pay | Document closure and rebooking |
| Client medical emergency | Waiver possible after review | Owner sets exceptional pay | Document decision privately |
| Admin moved session | No debit from change | No conducted pay from move | Reconfirm changed booking |
| Incorrect no-show deduction | Restore one through audit | No retroactive payout inferred | Resolve dispute and reconcile |
| Frozen package dates | No training | No conducted pay | Enforce approved freeze |
| Final booking not acknowledged | Do not charge a no-show | No automatic effect | Request member acknowledgment |

## 9. Evidence, privacy, retention and escalation

- Record **who, when, what, reason, and original/new state** for bookings, attendance and financial changes.
- Member sees own bookings, changes, credits and case decisions; Coach sees only assigned operational details; Admin sees authorized review evidence. Do not expose medical documents or private member dispute statements to coaches.
- Admin resolves disputes fairly and maintains original immutable payment/session sources. If a dispute impacts closed/paid payroll, document and handle as a new reviewed adjustment—never silently rewrite history.
- A manager/owner reviews unresolved discrepancies and future policy amendments. Changes apply prospectively with a versioned client notice.

## 10. Rollout and acceptance

These are the **required acceptance checks** before calling the policy foolproof:
1. Member requests, staff confirms, member sees and acknowledges final slot.
2. Staff moves coach/time; prior acknowledgment no longer counts; member must reconfirm.
3. Coach reports absence; package remains unchanged until Admin review.
4. Admin cannot charge when final booking was not acknowledged, terms were absent, or attendance conflicts.
5. Member sees reason for a staff/coach/gym cancellation, with no session debit.
6. Member can dispute and Admin can restore exactly once with an audit trail.
7. Coach payroll and historical member/payment/session records remain unchanged.
8. Two users concurrently attempting the same approval cannot double-charge or double-restore.
9. A coach cannot view another coach's roster, member medical evidence or member dispute narrative.
10. Test member, coach and Admin workflows using verified non-production accounts before broad rollout.

**Unresolved business policy:** coach compensation for nonconducted/shortened sessions must be separately signed by BearFit owner. This manual deliberately does not fabricate rates or override real client contracts.
